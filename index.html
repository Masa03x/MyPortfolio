01 — Create that UX

CZ healthcare

CZ was our client, and they wanted us to improve their Zorgvinder tool, focusing on mental healthcare. The current version is difficult to use, especially for people who struggle to know what to search for, how to compare providers, or how their insurance works. Our goal is to make Zorgvinder clearer, easier, and more supportive so clients can find the right mental healthcare.

02 — Branding

Digital Paradise

We worked to create a complete brand identity for our client, digital paradise, an electronic music producer and DJ. The assignment focused on translating his vision, values and cultural background into a strong visual identity and digital presence. Our work included research, stylescapes, a brand guide and a website prototype, with the goal of building a consistent brand that represents his music, community and energy across different platforms.
